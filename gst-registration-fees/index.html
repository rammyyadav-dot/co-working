Start with the right question

Know what you are paying for

Government application fee

GST registration on the official portal has no government application fee. A professional-service quote should not describe its consultancy charge as a mandatory GST registration fee.

Professional assistance

Consultant fees can cover applicability review, information preparation, document checks and agreed query responses. Ask whether follow-up work is included or separately billed.

Separate ongoing costs

Return filing, bookkeeping, digital signatures where relevant, address arrangements and later amendments may have separate costs. Registration support alone does not include every ongoing obligation.

Prepare your brief

Records to organise

  • Business constitution and principal business activity.
  • Proposed place of business and occupancy arrangement.
  • Proprietor, partner, director and authorised-signatory details as applicable.
  • Any existing application reference, query or additional place of business.

Indicative checklist. Use the actual portal instructions, communication and business circumstances to confirm the final requirements.

A practical route forward

What to do next

  1. Describe the business, location and any application already started.
  2. Confirm the scope: new registration, query assistance or a related service.
  3. Ask for consultant charges, taxes, inclusions and exclusions in writing.
  4. Approve the scope before payment; keep receipts and application records.

Keep this in view

Avoid comparing headline prices alone. Ask about document review, query responses, address-related services, applicable taxes and post-registration support.

Questions from Patna & Bihar businesses

Before you proceed

Is GST registration free in Patna?

The government does not charge an application fee for GST registration on the official portal. If you appoint a consultant, their professional charges are separate. Other business setup or ongoing compliance services may also be separately quoted.

What are JobReady’s GST registration charges?

Request a written quote after sharing the business type, premises arrangement and application status. No fixed GST-registration consultancy price is asserted on this page.

Are monthly GST returns included in registration fees?

Do not assume they are included. Agree the return periods, record requirements, filing scope and charges separately before engagement.
