Start with the right question

Make the premises record clear

Understand the communication

Read the application status, query or verification instruction carefully. Note what has been requested, the response channel and any stated deadline. Do not assume every application requires the same visit.

Prepare the premises evidence

Check that the business address, rental or ownership records, consent documents and supporting utility evidence are consistent. Requirements depend on the occupancy arrangement and current instructions.

Coordinate truthful access

Keep the actual business premises accessible through the responsible person as required. Explain the real activity and retain records; do not stage operations, fabricate documents or treat an address package as approval.

Prepare your brief

Records to organise

  • Application reference and the complete query or verification communication.
  • Ownership, rental/lease or consent records relevant to the occupancy type.
  • Required supporting premises evidence and consistent address details.
  • Business-activity details, authorisations and records requested by the officer.

Indicative checklist. Use the actual portal instructions, communication and business circumstances to confirm the final requirements.

A practical route forward

What to do next

  1. Review the actual status, communication and applicable response date.
  2. Reconcile premises details and identify missing supporting records.
  3. Follow the proper officer’s instructions for documents and access, where required.
  4. Keep the response acknowledgement and track subsequent portal updates.

Keep this in view

No consultant can guarantee inspection timing or approval. Follow the official communication, keep genuine records and request clarification through the appropriate channel when instructions are unclear.

Questions from Patna & Bihar businesses

Before you proceed

Does every GST application in Patna need physical verification?

Do not assume a universal requirement. Verification and authentication steps depend on the application and current rules or official instructions. Review your specific communication and status.

Can a rented shop be physically verified for GST?

The actual premises arrangement and required evidence need review. Keep occupancy documents and business details consistent, and follow the proper officer’s instructions; a rental agreement alone does not guarantee acceptance.

What should I do if my GST registration is pending?

Check the application status and communications for outstanding actions. Review queries, authentication and document gaps before choosing a technical-support or officer-response route.
