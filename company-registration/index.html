Company Registration in Patna: FAQs

Compare costs, documents and next steps before starting your application.

What are company registration fees in Patna, Bihar?

JobReady lists incorporation service plans from ₹1,999 plus government fees. The total depends on the entity, authorised capital, stamp duty, DSC costs, taxes and agreed scope. Ask for an itemised written quote before paying.

What documents are required for private limited company registration in Bihar?

Prepare PAN and accepted identity/address proofs as applicable, director and shareholder details, proposed names and business objects, and registered-office evidence. Rental or ownership records, owner consent/NOC and a recent utility bill may be required. Confirm the exact checklist for your application.

How do I choose between Pvt Ltd, LLP and OPC registration in Patna?

Compare the number and eligibility of owners, liability, fundraising plans, governance and recurring compliance. Pvt Ltd, LLP and OPC structures have different requirements; discuss your business model before selecting a route.

How long does company registration in Patna take?

Timing depends on complete documents, name acceptance, digital-signature verification, authority processing and any queries. Request an estimated filing schedule after your documents are reviewed; an approval date cannot be guaranteed.

Can I use a rented or virtual office for company registration in Bihar?

Review the actual premises arrangement, permitted use, owner consent and supporting records with your adviser. A rented or virtual-office package alone does not guarantee acceptance; the address and authorisation must support the application.

Does company registration include Startup Bihar or ₹10 lakh seed funding?

No. Incorporation, Startup Bihar recognition and funding evaluation are separate. Check the current Bihar Startup Policy for eligibility, the nature and ceiling of support, and sanction conditions. Registration does not guarantee funding.

Do I also need GST, Udyam or Shop and Establishment registration?

These registrations have different purposes and eligibility rules. Review your business activity, tax obligations, establishment and scheme requirements separately; company incorporation does not replace every other registration.
