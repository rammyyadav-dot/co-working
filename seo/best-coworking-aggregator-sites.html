03 / YOUR SHORTLIST

What to confirm before you commit

Use this checklist in a tour, email or provider conversation. Ask for specific answers that relate to your dates and proposed plan. Where a requirement is essential, have it recorded in the quotation or agreement.

  1. Check whether a platform takes payment, only sends leads or leaves booking entirely to the operator.
  2. Official location or verified operator identity
  3. Matching dates, capacity and workspace type
  4. Current quote, taxes and contract terms
  5. Payment recipient, support and cancellation owner
