04 / A useful answer

Before you decide

Does a proprietorship receive an MCA incorporation certificate?

No. Business evidence depends on applicable registrations and licences; it is not incorporated as a company through MCA.

Official references: Ministry of Corporate Affairs · Startup Bihar · Udyam official portal. Check current rules and portal instructions before filing.
