How does a virtual CFO differ from bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping records and reconciles transactions. A virtual CFO engagement adds agreed financial planning, reporting and decision support; statutory audit and certification are separate.

Official references: Income Tax Department · Bihar Commercial Taxes Department. Check current rules and portal instructions before filing.
