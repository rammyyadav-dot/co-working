Start with the right question

A practical drafting starting point

Identify the parties

Use the owner’s and applicant’s correct legal names and the complete premises address. Match details against the business application and supporting records.

State the permitted use

Describe the consent for the actual business and GST-registration use. The person signing must have authority to give consent for the premises.

Check the complete document set

Rental/lease evidence, ownership proof and utility records may be relevant depending on occupancy and current instructions. An NOC alone does not establish that every requirement is met.

Prepare your brief

Records to organise

  • Owner/consenting party details and authority to grant consent.
  • Applicant’s legal name and complete premises address.
  • Rental, ownership or consent arrangement as applicable.
  • Supporting records required for the chosen occupancy type.

Indicative checklist. Use the actual portal instructions, communication and business circumstances to confirm the final requirements.

A practical route forward

What to do next

  1. Confirm whether the premises are rented, owned, shared or used by consent.
  2. Prepare an accurate owner-consent draft using the real parties and address.
  3. Have the authorised owner/consenting party review and sign the appropriate document.
  4. Review the NOC together with all supporting premises records before filing.

Illustrative wording / review before use

Owner consent / no-objection letter: sample

Consent for use of premises for business and GST registration

I, [owner/authorised consenting party’s full name], confirm that I am authorised to grant consent for the premises at [complete premises address].

I have no objection to [applicant’s legal name] using the premises for [accurate business use] and using this address for GST registration, subject to our actual premises arrangement and applicable requirements.

This consent is given with the supporting premises records appropriate to the arrangement.

Place: [place]
Date: [date]
Name and signature: [authorised consenting party]
Contact details: [agreed contact details]

Bracketed fields are intentional sample fields. Confirm ownership/authority, permitted use and the supporting document set before signing or filing. No notarisation or stamp-paper rule is assumed by this sample.

Keep this in view

This illustrative wording is not an official prescribed format or a promise of acceptance. Do not invent rent, ownership, consent or utility details. Get the document set reviewed for your actual occupancy type.

Questions from Patna & Bihar businesses

Before you proceed

Is an NOC compulsory for every rented-shop GST application?

The required documents depend on the premises arrangement and current portal instructions. Review whether owner consent/NOC and other records are needed in your case; do not apply one checklist to every occupancy type.

Does an NOC replace a rent agreement or utility bill?

Do not assume it does. The complete document set depends on the occupancy basis and instructions. Review consent, rental/ownership evidence and supporting premises records together.

Can I copy this GST NOC sample and submit it unchanged?

No. Replace every illustrative field with accurate details, confirm the signatory’s authority and have the wording and supporting records reviewed for your specific application.
