Prepare for the first review

Your tender-document checklist.

Share the public tender reference and the support you need first. Use an agreed secure channel for private records after the scope is confirmed.

  • Tender ID, portal link, complete bid document and all available corrigenda.
  • Submission date and time, pre-bid meeting details and clarification deadlines.
  • Business constitution and tax details required by the tender.
  • Financial statements, turnover evidence and relevant experience records.
  • Technical specifications, authorisations, declarations and proposed compliance responses.
  • EMD, exemption or performance-security requirements and supporting evidence.

Check before upload

Six avoidable document gaps

  • Names or addresses differ between records.
  • Certificates do not cover the required period.
  • Experience evidence does not match the stated definition.
  • Mandatory formats, signatures or declarations are missing.
  • A BOQ template is altered outside permitted fields.
  • Bid-security validity or exemption evidence is incomplete.

The final checklist is always tender-specific. Your adviser should review exceptions against the actual conditions.

Tender consultancy / local questions

Know the scope before you bid.

What does a government tender consultant in Patna help with?

The scope can include tender eligibility review, GeM seller onboarding guidance, a document checklist, technical bid preparation, BOQ review, EMD/compliance tracking and post-award billing records. Agree the deliverables for your specific tender before engagement.

Can you help with GeM registration and bid participation in Bihar?

You can enquire about GeM seller registration guidance, profile or catalogue readiness and bid-document support. Registration and bid participation have different requirements. The seller remains responsible for accurate declarations, credentials and final authorisation.

What documents are needed for a Bihar government tender?

Requirements come from the tender and its corrigenda. Common review areas include business identity, GST/PAN details as applicable, financial statements, turnover or experience evidence, authorisations, declarations and bid-security records. Do not assume one checklist fits every department or bid.

Are MSMEs or Udyam-registered businesses always exempt from EMD?

No blanket exemption should be assumed. Check the exact tender conditions, applicable procurement rules, bidder category and evidence required. Udyam registration alone should not be treated as automatic acceptance of an exemption claim.

Can a consultant guarantee a tender win or government payment?

No. Eligibility, technical evaluation, pricing, award and payment decisions remain with the procuring entity under the applicable conditions. Consultancy supports preparation and record control; it does not guarantee selection, release of payment or exemption approval.

What are tender consultancy fees in Patna?

Fees depend on the portal, document readiness, number of lots or schedules, technical complexity, clarifications and post-award scope. Request a written quote showing professional charges, taxes, exclusions and any separate portal or bid-security costs.

Do you support contractors, medical suppliers and IT vendors?

Enquiries can cover civil works, goods supply and IT or service procurement. Sector-specific experience, licences, manufacturer authorisations and technical evidence must be checked against the tender. Confirm the team’s relevant capability and agreed scope before appointing them.

What does post-award billing support cover?

An agreed scope may include organising purchase orders or contracts, delivery or acceptance records, invoice checklists, payment-status trackers and performance-security reminders. Works measurements, technical certification and approvals remain with the responsible authorised parties.

Can you submit a tender without sharing my DSC password or OTP?

Discuss an authorised workflow in which the bidder controls its digital signature, authentication and final approval. Do not send passwords, OTPs, DSC PINs, private keys or confidential bid prices through an initial enquiry form.
