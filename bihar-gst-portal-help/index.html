Start with the right question

Know where to ask

GST portal technical issue

For login, system errors or status problems, start with the support options on the official GST portal. Record the screen, exact error and time before raising a ticket.

Bihar department contact

Use the Bihar Commercial Taxes Department website to verify current contact details and departmental notices. The appropriate office or officer may depend on your registration and jurisdiction.

Document or filing question

An application query, tax notice or document mismatch may require a substantive response rather than a technical ticket. Share the communication type and deadline with your adviser.

Prepare your brief

Records to organise

  • Portal name, page or module and the action that failed.
  • Exact error message, date and time, and a redacted screenshot if useful.
  • Application reference or GSTIN through an agreed channel, where needed.
  • Ticket reference, previous responses and any filing or notice deadline.

Indicative checklist. Use the actual portal instructions, communication and business circumstances to confirm the final requirements.

A practical route forward

What to do next

  1. Identify the portal and classify the problem: technical, application or statutory.
  2. Check official support options and prepare a concise issue description.
  3. Raise or update the appropriate ticket; retain the acknowledgement.
  4. Track the response separately from any filing or notice obligation.

Keep this in view

A portal ticket is not a deadline extension. If a return, query or notice has a response date, discuss the required action while the technical issue is being reviewed.

Questions from Patna & Bihar businesses

Before you proceed

What is the Bihar GST helpline number?

Check the current number on the Bihar Commercial Taxes Department website or the official GST portal support section. This page does not publish an unverified helpline number; JobReady’s contact number is a consultancy enquiry line, not a government helpline.

Can JobReady access my GST login for me?

Agree an authorised workflow before any account action. Do not put passwords or OTPs in the enquiry form. Keep authentication and final approval under the taxpayer’s control.

Should I contact GST portal support or my jurisdictional officer?

Technical portal faults and officer-issued queries are different issues. Use the communication, module and issue type to identify the right route; confirm current jurisdiction through official records.
